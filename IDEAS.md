# IDEAS.md

Things worth building, parked deliberately.

**The point of writing an idea down is to stop thinking about it.** An idea living in your
head during Phase 3 is a distraction; the same idea on this page is an option.

Record *why* something was dropped. A dropped idea with reasoning is as useful as a built
one — it's the difference between "I didn't think of it" and "I considered it and here's
the trade."

Status: `PARKED` (might build) · `DROPPED` (decided against) · `BUILT` (moved into a phase)
